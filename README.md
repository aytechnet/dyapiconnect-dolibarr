# DyaPiConnect for [Dolibarr ERP CRM](https://www.dolibarr.org)

DyaPiConnect links your Dolibarr to the [DyaPi](https://dyapi.io) micro-services, which interconnect
your e-commerce / booking software with Dolibarr and other platforms.

## E-commerce &harr; Dolibarr

Synchronise orders, invoices, shipments, stock and payments between Dolibarr and an e-commerce
(PrestaShop, WooCommerce…).
Pick a setup mode (Minimalist, Propale, Invoice, E-Commerce) to pre-activate the right Dolibarr modules.

## French electronic invoicing (Factur-X)

When a customer invoice is validated, DyaPiConnect asks DyaPi to turn its PDF into a conformant
**Factur-X (PDF/A-3, EN 16931)** invoice and stores it back in the invoice. The seller identity
(company name, SIREN/SIRET, VAT, address, bank IBAN) is sourced automatically from your Dolibarr at
registration and kept in sync — you manage it in Dolibarr, never twice. Transmission to a certified
platform (PDP) is the next step, for the French e-invoicing mandate (reception Sept. 2026, emission
2026–2027).

## Privacy

Your DyaPi identifiers (API key, secret) stay on this server and are never displayed. Connect with
**Link to my DyaPi account** from the module setup page: you sign in on DyaPi and confirm the link.

## Translations

Translations can be completed by editing the files in the *langs* directory.
